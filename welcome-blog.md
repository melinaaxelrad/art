Welcome to my website! I'm Melina Axelrad, a 21-year-old artist based in Arlington, Virginia.

## My Inspiration

My passion for painting comes from my late grandmother, Florence-ann, who was an exceptional multimedia artist. She always encouraged my creative endeavors and taught me to see the beauty in everyday scenes.

## My Process

I primarily work with oil paints, which allow me to achieve rich color saturation and smooth blending. My work explores lighting and shapes in landscapes, from intimate backyard scenes to expansive mountain vistas and ocean views.

Each painting is a meditation on place and memory, capturing moments of light and atmosphere that feel both familiar and transcendent.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope my work resonates with you.
