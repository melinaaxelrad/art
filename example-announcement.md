I'm thrilled to announce that my latest collection of landscape paintings will be on display at the Arlington Arts Center starting February 1st, 2025.

The exhibition, titled "From Backyard to Mountains," features works that explore the relationship between familiar local scenes and distant vistas. Each piece captures the unique lighting and atmospheric conditions that define these different landscapes.

The opening reception will be held on February 1st from 6-8 PM. I hope to see you there!
